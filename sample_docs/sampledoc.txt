GOVERNMENT OF MAHARASHTRA
General Administration Department
Circular No. GAD/2024/CR-45/M-1
Date: 15th January 2024

Subject: Implementation of Digital Record Keeping in all Government Offices

All District Collectors and Heads of Departments are hereby directed to:

1. Implement digital record keeping systems in their offices by 31st March 2024.
2. Appoint a nodal officer for digital records by 28th February 2024.
3. Submit a compliance report to GAD by 5th April 2024.
4. Ensure all staff complete digital literacy training by 31st March 2024.

Authority: Chief Secretary, Maharashtra
Penalty for non-compliance: Action under Maharashtra Civil Services Rules.

Issued by: General Administration Department
